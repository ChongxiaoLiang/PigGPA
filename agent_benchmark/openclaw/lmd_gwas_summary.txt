=======================================================================
CONCLUSIONS
=======================================================================
1. Loin muscle depth is highly polygenic (201 independent loci, λ=2.95).
2. Most top loci exhibit regulatory features (lncRNAs, miRNAs, gene-dense
   regions) predicting active chromatin states.
3. The Chr10:27.1 Mb miRNA cluster (miR-23b/27b/24-2) is the most compelling
   functional candidate based on known muscle regulatory biology.
4. Chr4 harbors two strong loci with muscle-relevant genes (DEPTOR, CCN3, EIF3H).
5. Chr11 contains two lncRNA-rich regulatory hotspots.
6. The loci do not overlap known major pig muscle QTL genes, suggesting
   they represent novel genetic architecture for loin muscle depth.
7. Recommended follow-up: functional validation of the Chr10 miRNA cluster
   and the Chr4 DEPTOR/CCN3 locus in muscle cell lines.
